Our Story

North Star Bakery began as a family kitchen project and grew into a neighborhood gathering place. We bake the way our grandparents did: by hand, with patience, and with enough to share.

Every morning our doors open to neighbors, commuters picking up breakfast, and families stocking up for the weekend.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

  • Locally milled flour whenever we can get it
  • Real butter, eggs from nearby farms, and no artificial preservatives
  • Seasonal fruit and ingredients from neighborhood growers
